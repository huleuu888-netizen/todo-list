## W1 — F06 dam shell gravel

- **Warning content:** `SOLVER PROBLEM. NUMERICAL SINGULARITY WHEN PROCESSING NODE P25_SOLID_DAM_SHELL_GRAVEL_F06-1.1185 D.O.F. 3 RATIO = 154.800E+12`.
- **Source:** `v15_15_S00_SOLVER_CLOSURE.msg`; the successful completion and zero-cutback evidence are in the same `.msg` and `.sta`.
- **Likely root cause:** local mechanical conditioning in the porous F06 gravel instance, such as a soft or highly distorted local element configuration. The files do not prove one unique geometric cause; this is a diagnosis for later targeted mesh/constitutive review, not a license to change the material.
- **Effect on seepage:** no zero pivot or pore-pressure DOF 8 singularity was reported. S00 produced POR and FLVEL fields and completed. The warning can still affect locally coupled displacement/volume response, so results requiring strong hydro-mechanical interpretation should remain qualified.
- **Severity:** `MEDIUM` for the existing baseline hydraulic field; `MEDIUM/HIGH` if mechanical response is used for production interpretation.
- **Modification decision:** `NO` in V15.17. Do not add a tie, fixed node, spring, or arbitrary material change. Defer a local mesh-quality and mechanical-conditioning study.

## W2 — F10 upstream gravel fill

- **Warning content:** `SOLVER PROBLEM. NUMERICAL SINGULARITY WHEN PROCESSING NODE P25_SOLID_UPSTREAM_GRAVEL_FILL_F10-1.1051 D.O.F. 3 RATIO = 42.9852E+12`.
- **Source:** `v15_15_S00_SOLVER_CLOSURE.msg`; completion is corroborated by `v15_15_S00_SOLVER_CLOSURE.sta`.
- **Likely root cause:** local ill-conditioning in the F10 porous upstream-fill mechanics. It is not evidence of a missing hydraulic boundary condition: S00 has no zero-pivot failure and reaches the final increment.
- **Effect on seepage:** no direct pore-pressure equation failure is shown. The possible influence is indirect through the coupled mechanical state, so the baseline is valid as a solver-completed reference but not as a proof that all local hydro-mechanical quantities are fully conditioned.
- **Severity:** `MEDIUM`.
- **Modification decision:** `NO` in V15.17. Keep the warning visible and address only through a separately justified local mesh/material sensitivity study.

## W3 — F11 main rockfill

- **Warning content:** `SOLVER PROBLEM. NUMERICAL SINGULARITY WHEN PROCESSING NODE P25_SOLID_MAIN_ROCKFILL_F11-1.386 D.O.F. 3 RATIO = 27.6199E+12`.
- **Source:** `v15_15_S00_SOLVER_CLOSURE.msg`; the final step completed with zero cutbacks according to the `.msg` and `.sta`.
- **Likely root cause:** local mechanical stiffness conditioning in the F11 rockfill instance. The V15.15 input keeps the existing formulation and does not supply a basis for inventing a new rock permeability or stiffness value.
- **Effect on seepage:** the warning is on mechanical DOF 3, not hydraulic DOF 8. It did not prevent POR/FLVEL output or completion of S00, but it limits confidence in any interpretation that depends on local deformation-induced hydraulic changes.
- **Severity:** `MEDIUM`.
- **Modification decision:** `NO` in V15.17. Preserve the verified anti-seepage system and current parameter values.

## W4 — F09 main rockfill

- **Warning content:** `SOLVER PROBLEM. NUMERICAL SINGULARITY WHEN PROCESSING NODE P25_SOLID_MAIN_ROCKFILL_F09-1.1370 D.O.F. 3 RATIO = 1.27096E+15`.
- **Source:** `v15_15_S00_SOLVER_CLOSURE.msg`; `v15_15_S00_SOLVER_CLOSURE.sta` states `THE ANALYSIS HAS COMPLETED SUCCESSFULLY`.
- **Likely root cause:** the largest conditioning ratio occurs in the porous F09 rockfill mechanical DOF. The available audit trail cannot distinguish local mesh distortion from a local stiffness contrast without a new targeted study; no such study is authorized in V15.17.
- **Effect on seepage:** no direct hydraulic singularity or failed increment is recorded. The final baseline POR and FLVEL fields exist, but F09 is the strongest reason not to label the coupled result fully production-ready for mechanically sensitive interpretation.
- **Severity:** `HIGH` for coupled hydro-mechanical interpretation; `MEDIUM` for the completed baseline hydraulic reference.
- **Modification decision:** `NO` in V15.17. Do not suppress the warning or cure it with artificial constraints or unverified parameter changes.

## Other S00 warnings (not counted as the four numerical records)

The `.msg` also reports 14 unconnected regions. V15.15 records these as the
14 retained separate hydraulic instances; unsupported C3D8R-only structural
instances are inactive in S00. The `.dat` additionally reports 26,069 distorted
elements, 1,298 elements with aspect ratio above 100:1, ignored steady-state
pore-pressure change output, unavailable `FLVEL` on C3D8R elements, and many
inactive DOF 8 notices on structural nodes. These remain traceable warnings;
they are not reclassified as the four solver numerical-problem records.

## V15.17 disposition

`Geometry Solver Readiness` remains inherited as `PASS`, and S00 remains
`COMPLETED_WITH_NUMERICAL_WARNINGS`. The four warnings are documented, not
deleted. No geometry/topology, constraint, boundary-condition, or material
parameter modification is justified by the current evidence. Production
S01-S07 remains blocked pending engineering parameter closure and a decision on
whether the mechanical conditioning warnings are acceptable for the intended
production interpretation.
