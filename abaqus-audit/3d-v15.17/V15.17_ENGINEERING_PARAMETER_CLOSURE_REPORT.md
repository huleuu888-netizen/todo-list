instance, node label, DOF, ratio, source file, hydraulic impact, severity, and
modification decision. They are retained as warnings. The separate 14-region

rock materials without invented permeability values and lists the existing
active porous geological coefficients. The existing coefficients are classified
as engineering assumptions pending direct evidence or inverse calibration.

`UNRESOLVED` and records the verified geometry / unverified parameter status of
the retained anti-seepage regions. It does not create a curtain or change its
geometry.

equivalent mapping from the alluvial geology mapping. The current density and
permeability are recorded from the existing model, not changed to force a
solver result, and both uses remain calibration-required.
