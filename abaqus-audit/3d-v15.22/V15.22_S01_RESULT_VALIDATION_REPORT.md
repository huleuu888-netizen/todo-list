## Extraction method

The script opens the ODB read-only and inspects the final frame and the step
history regions. It reports values only when the requested quantity is
explicitly represented by an ODB field or history output. `FLVEL` is retained
as a velocity diagnostic, but it is not treated as discharge. `POR` is retained
as pore-pressure evidence, but it is not converted to water head without a
documented unit and elevation convention. No Q, head, or gradient value is
inferred from unrelated fields.
